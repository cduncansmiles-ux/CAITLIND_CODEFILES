About Us

HOW DID OUR STORY BEGIN?

Our Journey

NESTLED IN THE BUSTLING HEART OF AUCKLAND CITY, HONEY & CUP BEGAN AS A LITTLE DREAM IN 2018, LOVINGLY BUILT FROM THE GROUND UP BY A LOCAL FAMILY WITH A PASSION FOR COMFORTING AROMAS AND GENUINE COMMUNITY. WHAT STARTED AS BARE CONCRETE WALLS AND A SHARED VISION OF A WARM SANCTUARY SOON TRANSFORMED INTO A BELOVED NEIGHBORHOOD HAVEN, SCENTED WITH THE RICH NOTES OF ARTISANAL ESPRESSO AND FRESHLY BAKED MANUKA-HONEY BRIOCHE. TODAY, THIS COZY CORNER CAFÉ STANDS AS A CHERISHED URBAN RETREAT WHERE RAIN-SOAKED COMMUTERS AND LINGERING DREAMERS ALIKE FIND REFUGE, WRAPPED IN THE GOLDEN GLOW OF FAIRY LIGHTS, VINTAGE WOODEN BOOTHS, AND A STEADFAST DEDICATION TO MAKING EVERY CUP FEEL LIKE COMING HOME.



OUR STORY ISN'T JUST ABOUT COFFEE; IT'S ABOUT CONNECTION, COMMUNITY, AND CREATING A SPACE WHERE EVERYONE FEELS WELCOME. FROM THE FIRST SIP TO THE LAST CRUMB OF OUR HONEY-INFUSED TREATS, WE INVITE YOU TO BE PART OF OUR JOURNEY, TO SHARE IN THE LAUGHTER, THE STORIES, AND THE SIMPLE PLEASURES THAT MAKE HONEY & CUP MORE THAN JUST A CAFÉ—IT'S A PLACE WHERE MEMORIES ARE BREWED AND FRIENDSHIPS ARE FOSTERED.
